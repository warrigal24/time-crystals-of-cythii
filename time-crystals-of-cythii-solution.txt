The Time Crystals of Cythii
By Garry Francis
For z-code interpreters

Solution by Garry Francis
----------------------------------------
This is a minimal solution. You'll need to EXAMINE lots of things for this solution to make sense. Also, TALK TO non-player characters and ASK ABOUT anything they mention that sounds interesting or may be relevant. The game is chock full of clues and includes a HINT command if you get desperate. Optional (but significant) moves are shown in parentheses. These can be omitted once you know what to do.

The game starts in the present at 9:00 a.m. in the Time Tower of Cythii. There are five other time periods, each ending in a disaster:
- London starts at 12:15 a.m., ends at 1:00 a.m., giving you 45 minutes.
- Krakatoa starts at 4:10 a.m., ends at 5:30 a.m., giving you 80 minutes.
- San Francisco starts at 4:22 a.m., ends at 5:12 a.m., giving you 50 minutes.
- Titanic starts at 1:30 a.m., ends at 2:20 a.m., giving you 50 minutes.
- Hindenburg starts at 6:45 p.m., ends at 7:25 p.m., giving you 40 minutes.

Each move takes one minute.

When you leave the time tower and return, the time continues on from where it left off. Whereas, when you leave the time tower and enter another time period, the time restarts at the start of that time period. You can use this to your advantage. Once you've worked out where you are, the game will tell you how long you've got until the disaster occurs, so keep your eye on the time in the status bar or on your watch. When the disaster is imminent, you will start getting warning messages. When the warning messages tell you to "get back to the time tower, NOW!", make sure you follow the advice or you'll get killed in the disaster. Therefore, if you're running out of time, just leave the time period and return.

You must visit London and the Hindenburg at least once and the other time periods at least twice. However, you may need to visit each time period multiple times until you have fully explored those time periods and know what to do.

The solution assumes you play with the tutorial turned on and follow all the suggestions. These are shown as optional moves.
